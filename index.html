About

I am an Associate Professor at Süleyman Demirel University whose research lies at the intersection of transportation engineering, intelligent transportation systems and AI-based analytics.

My research explores how traffic systems behave, how their performance can be improved, and how data-driven models can support reliable and sustainable transportation decisions.
